How to play

Advance through all seven levels of the Crawsword. Each level is a single five-letter crossword answer, and you get 5 guesses for each level. A guess is only used up if your answer isn't correct, so be careful!

The clues get harder as you climb, just like the daily crossword gets harder as the week goes on. Run out of guesses on a level and the answer is revealed so you can keep going — but solve all seven yourself for a Flawless finish!

After each guess, the color of the tiles changes to show how close you were to the answer.

When you solve a level, one green letter carries over and is given to you in the next answer — a free correct letter to build on.
